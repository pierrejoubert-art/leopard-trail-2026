  7. Paste the values into the FIREBASE_CONFIG object in index.html

ℹ️ About the Leopard Trail

The Leopard Trail is a 4-day slackpacking hike through the spectacular Baviaanskloof World Heritage Site in the Eastern Cape. Your heavy bags are transported between camps — you hike with just a daypack.

  • Sleeping huts at each camp (4 people per hut, mattresses provided)
  • Communal cooking hut with gas stoves at each camp
  • Self-catering — bring your own food
  • Optional Day 4: Cedar Falls hike from base camp (~8 km return)

Easy start along Rhebok Valley, then up to the first real saddle with spectacular views of the Kouga and Baviaans foothills. A varied day across the landscape before arriving at the overnight huts. Allow 5–8 hours.

🥾 Day 3 — Wed 10 Jun

The longest and most challenging day — tackle the three climbs of Honeybush Hill, Inconvenient Truth and Ain't so Bad. True wilderness with views deep into the Baviaans and Kouga mountains. The second half descends through the beautiful Kasey Kloof. Allow 6–9 hours.

🥾 Day 4 — Thu 11 Jun

Camp 3 → Cedar Falls Base Camp

Final day on trail — follow Birdsong Valley, then climb Fond Farewell to the plateau. Descend into the Cedar Falls valley and arrive at base camp for a traditional swim in the reservoir. Celebrate finishing the trail with a braai! Overnight at Cedar Falls Base Camp (✅ confirmed).

🐆 Leopard Trail Basics

🎒Slackpacking — your heavy bags are transported to the next camp. You hike with a daypack containing water, lunch, snacks, rain gear, and a warm layer. Pack your overnight bag separately in a duffel/bag that will be moved for you.
🛖Sleeping huts have mattresses — bring a sleeping bag (rated for cold) and a pillow/liner. No need to carry a tent!
🍳Communal cooking hut at each camp has gas stoves. Bring your own food, pots, and utensils. The cooking hut is shared with other groups (up to 12 hikers total).

🥶 Winter Weather (June)

🌡️June = winter in SA. Expect daytime temps of 15–22°C and nights potentially down to 0–5°C at altitude. Frost is possible. Layer up — warm thermals and a proper fleece are essential.
🌧️Eastern Cape winter is generally dry but cold fronts can bring rain. Pack a rain jacket and waterproofs just in case. Shorter days: sunrise ~07:10, sunset ~17:25 — plan your hiking accordingly.

💧 Water

💧Carry at least 2–3L in your daypack. June is winter so streams should be flowing, but confirm conditions with Go Baviaans before your trip. Fill up at every source along the trail.

🐆 Wildlife

🐆Leopards are present in the Baviaanskloof — hence the name! Sightings are extremely rare. Trail cameras have captured them. You're far more likely to see their tracks than the cats themselves.
🐒Baboons can be bold. Never leave food unattended at camp. Secure your bags when you leave.
🐍Snakes: Cape cobra and puff adder are present. Watch where you step and place your hands, especially on rocky terrain in the afternoon sun.

🧭 Navigation

🗺️The trail is well-marked with cairns and paint blazes, but carry a map and phone with offline GPS as backup. Cell signal is very limited to non-existent in the Baviaanskloof.

🚗 Getting There

📍Cedar Falls Base Camp is in the Baviaanskloof, Eastern Cape. Access via the R332 (Baviaanskloof road) — can take 4x4 roads in places. Check road conditions before departure. Allow plenty of driving time.
⛽Fill up fuel before entering the Baviaanskloof. There are no fuel stations along the way. Last fuel: Graaff-Reinet, Willowmore, or Patensie depending on your approach route.

📦 Slackpack Bag Tips

⚖️Your slackpack bag can be a duffel or backpack — it doesn't need to be hiking-friendly since it's transported. Put sleeping bag, camp clothes, food supplies, cooking gear, and extras in here.
🎒Your daypack should be 20–30L max. In it: water (2–3L), lunch, snacks, rain jacket, warm layer, sunscreen, hat, first aid, phone, power bank.

🔥 Fires & Cooking

🔥Check with Go Baviaans about fire rules at base camp — braais may be allowed at Cedar Falls. On the trail, use the communal cooking hut gas stoves only.

🏞️ Cedar Falls (Day 4)

💦A beautiful ~8 km return hike from base camp to a stunning waterfall. Easy and flat terrain. A perfect way to close out the trip before driving home.
